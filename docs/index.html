Recordo is a macOS menu bar app. It turns the terms you ask Claude about into four-choice quizzes, then asks you again as you start to forget.

  1. You ask Claude Code what a term means.
  2. At its daily sync, Recordo turns the answer into a card.
  3. When the card is due, Recordo quizzes you in the corner of your screen.

How it works

1

Ask as usual

Ask Claude Code what a word or abbreviation means, in a normal prompt or with /btw. To skip the filtering step, ask with /term LCP.

2

Recordo writes the cards

Once a day, Recordo has Claude pick out your new prompts that ask what something means. Claude then writes a short definition from that conversation, plus three wrong answers that sound right.

3

A quiz in the corner

Three due cards (you can set up to 10) show up in a small panel at the bottom right of your screen, at most once every three hours by default. Pick an answer and go back to what you were doing.

Reviews timed to the forgetting curve

Recordo spaces reviews along Ebbinghaus’s forgetting curve and asks again as a term starts to fade, so it sticks. Each correct answer moves the card up a box, and it waits longer before it’s due again.

Recordo waits for a good moment

Your keyboard stays put

The panel shows up without taking focus. Your keystrokes still go to the app you were using.

Quiet when you’re busy

Automatic quizzes skip your Quiet Hours, Focus, and times when the app in front is full screen. Recordo can’t see a Focus that starts on a schedule, so cover those hours with Quiet Hours.

Put it off

“Later” closes the panel, and the same cards come back at the next quiz.

Drop bad cards

If Claude got a definition wrong, discard the card after you answer.

Every card in one window

Search terms and definitions, see when each card comes back, and discard the ones you don’t need. Press ⌃⌥⌘C to open it from any app.

Your cards stay on your Mac

Recordo keeps your cards on your Mac, in one JSON file in your Application Support folder. During a sync, it sends your new prompts, parts of the conversations around your questions, and your existing card tags to Claude through your own claude CLI. Recordo has no account or server of its own, and it runs the CLI with tools, hooks, and MCP servers turned off.

Install

Build Recordo from source. You need macOS 14 or later, a Swift 6 toolchain (Xcode or the Command Line Tools), and the Claude Code CLI, logged in.

git clone https://github.com/impelcrypto/recordo.git
cd recordo
make install        # build, copy to /Applications, and launch
make install-skill  # optional: adds the /term skill
  • Recordo lives in the menu bar and has no Dock icon. The first launch syncs your last 30 days of questions in the background, which takes about 30 minutes. A percentage next to the menu bar icon shows how far along it is.
  • Recordo works in English and Japanese.
  • To pause quizzes during Focus, give Recordo Full Disk Access. A button on the Quizzes page in Settings opens the right pane in System Settings.
